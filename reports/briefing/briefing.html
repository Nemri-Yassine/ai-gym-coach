AI Workout Coach
Briefing · validation tomorrow
Most apps tell you what you did.
Ours tells you when to stop.

An AI coach that watches you train with your phone camera and your smartwatch. It recognizes the exercise, checks your form, compares today with your own past sessions, and tells you to stop before fatigue turns into injury.

3The 4 research questions, in plain words

RQ1

Does it recognize the exercise and count reps on a person it has never seen?

A coach that only works on its training people is useless.

Answer looks like: "92 % correct on new people, 0.3 reps off per set."

RQ2

Does the watch's heart rate detect fatigue better than movement alone?

The teacher asked us to use the watch's vital data. We train Model 3 with and without heart rate.

"With heart rate, the warning comes 2 reps earlier." Or "it doesn't help". Both are results.

RQ3

Is comparing you with your own past sessions better than comparing you with an average person?MAIN IDEA

People are very different; one threshold can't fit everyone.

Your squats are usually 1.4 s, today 1.8 s → warning, even if 1.8 s is normal for someone else.

RQ4

Does it still work on our own phone and Galaxy Watch 7, outside the dataset?

The "useful in real life" test.

"90 % on the dataset, 80 % on our devices: we lose 10 % because of camera quality and angle."

6When the teacher says…, we answer

"Comparing live frames to the dataset is not training."
We train 3 models ourselves: recognition, form check, fatigue and risk.
"Camera + watch beats watch alone, that's obvious."
Agreed. It's only a sanity check, not our claim. Our claims are RQ2 (heart rate) and RQ3 (personal history).
"Posture detection already exists."
Yes, so we reuse MediaPipe for it and spend our training on fatigue and risk.
"Who guarantees the reps in the dataset are correct?"
EC3D: every mistake was performed on instruction and each sequence was checked by hand. MM-Fit is never used to judge form.
"You have no injury labels."
Right, and we never claim to predict injuries. We detect fatigue and form breakdown, the warning signs, and recommend stopping.
"EC3D has only 4 people."
We test the strictest way (train on 3, test on the 4th), augment with 3D views from any angle, and add our own reps as a test set.
"3.8 GB is not big data."
70.8 M rows already, and the hard part is speed and variety: 4 live streams at 1–100 Hz to sync, history growing every session.
"Two people is not a dataset."
It's a proof of concept: each of us is compared with our own history. No public dataset follows one person for weeks.
