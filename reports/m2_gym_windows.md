## Reading (hand-written summary, 2026-11-21)

- **Coverage:** Model 2 now judges 7 exercises on real gym people (before: squat only). Mean balanced accuracy is about 0.68 per window on unseen people. Triceps is the best (0.75), rows the weakest (0.60).
- **Set accuracy is about the same as window accuracy (0.69).** Errors are whole people, not random moments: one absolute line does not fit 26 different bodies and setups.
- **Relative judgement is the fix:** ranking a person's two sets is right about 82% of the time (control on body proportions only: 0.58). The live demo now compares each set with the person's usual (median of earlier sets of the exercise) and flags a drop of 0.10 or more. Measured: bad sets caught 67% (Mendeley), our own correct sets flagged 17% (18 pairs; absolute verdict about 35%).
- **Personal calibration failed (leak):** features minus a reference from the start of the good set looked +1.5 points better, but reference and good test windows share one recording. On our own new sets it called only 23% correct. Switched off (`USE_CALIBRATION = False`).
- **Shortcut control:** body proportions alone give 0.54 (absolute) and 0.58 (relative), so the models mostly learn movement, not camera placement.
- **Open problems:** (1) a fair calibration needs two good sets of the same person, which Mendeley does not have; (2) no bad-form recording of our own yet, so detection on our camera is untested; (3) mistake type (knees in, not low…) still only from EC3D.
