03 · Step 1 · We built the recording station

A real gym, a phone on a tripod, a watch on the wrist

  • The iPhone films in Safari and uploads each set live to the laptop. No cable, no file transfer.
  • The watch heart rate goes to the laptop over Bluetooth.
  • The laptop is the control screen: exercise, start / end, reps, effort, notes.
  • Phone and laptop clocks are synced, so video, heart rate and labels line up.

07 · Where it works

Two people, two exercises, correct live

Squats · Yassine
Push-ups · Iheb

These exercises look the same in MM-Fit and in our gym: same movement, a camera that sees the whole body. The model decides after a few seconds of movement, then counts the reps.

08 · A useful surprise

We pressed "Squats". We did push-ups. The model said push-ups.

3 wrong buttons in our labels

  • Squats → really push-ups: model right
  • Lateral raises → really bicep curls: model right
  • Squats → really triceps: model no exercise

The model can check the labels a person types. That's how we found the mistakes; the corrections themselves come from our written notes.

09 · Where it fails, and why

The 4 failures are systematic: all 3 of our models fail on the same sets

10 · Proof: our movement vs the training data

Our variant moves differently from MM-Fit's version

  • Triceps: ours is a cable push-down near the hip; MM-Fit's is overhead.
  • Lateral raises: same arm arc as a jumping jack.
  • Sit-ups: legs flat on the floor; looks like a push-up from the side.
  • Rows: one arm, bent over, side view; the low arm looks like a push-up arm.

Mean joint height over one rep: our sets vs MM-Fit's true class vs the class the model picked.

15 · What we learned · what's next

The models work where our gym looks like their data.
Where it doesn't, we know why and how to fix it.

Learned

  • 5 exercises transfer perfectly to a phone in a real gym
  • Failures come from a different variant or camera view, not from noise
  • A few of our own videos fix part of it (75% → 92%)
  • Form models trained on motion capture don't transfer without calibration

Limits (honest)

  • 20 sets, 2 people, 1 day: small
  • No lunges yet; triceps variant differs
  • Watch heart rate ≈ 50% coverage
  • Rep counts being re-checked by hand

Week 3

  • Longer sessions to real fatigue (RPE 8–9)
  • Model 3: fatigue risk on our own data
  • More people; adapt on our variants
  • Calibrate the form model on our camera
