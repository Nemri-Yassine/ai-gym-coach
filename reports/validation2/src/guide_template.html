How week 1 works, and why we built it this way

Our own notes for the validation. Each part follows the presentation: what goes in, what comes out, the parameters that matter, and the reason behind each choice. Every number comes from the code and the logged experiments.

0

The whole flow

Two paths share the same building blocks. Training reads the MM-Fit dataset. Live use reads a camera. Both turn the person into the same 18-joint skeleton, so a model trained on the dataset works on a new video.

1

Data and splits

DatasetMM-Fit: 10 people, 21 workout sessions, 10 exercises, 6,160 labelled reps.
Streams we useCamera skeleton (30 fps), left-wrist watch motion (100 Hz), watch heart rate (~1 Hz).
LabelsPer set: start frame, end frame, rep count, exercise. Frames outside a set are "no activity".
SplitSessionsUsed for
trainw01 w02 w03 w04 w06 w07 w08 w16 w17 w18learning the weights
validationw14 w15 w19early stopping, choosing the best epoch
test, seen peoplew09 w10 w11people also present in training
test, unseen peoplew00 w05 w12 w13 w20our main number: people never seen

Why this split: it is the one the MM-Fit authors use in their own code, so our numbers can be compared with theirs. Testing on unseen people is the honest test: a coach must work on a new user, not only on the people it learned from.

2

Spark sync

InputPer session: pose (2, frames, 19), watch acc and gyro rows [frame, time, x, y, z], heart-rate rows [frame, time, bpm].
OutputParquet, one row per video frame: 36 joint coordinates, 6 watch values, heart rate, heart-rate mask, exercise, set id. 1,460,366 rows.
Codesrc/awc/features/sync.py (logic) and src/awc/bigdata/build_interim.py (Spark job).
ParameterValueWhy
Join keyframe numberEvery MM-Fit file already carries the video frame, so no time alignment guesswork is needed.
Reference clockcamera, 30 fpsThe camera is our main live sensor; everything is expressed on its clock.
Watch motionmean per frame (~3.3 samples)Reduces 100 Hz to 30 Hz without losing the movement shape.
Heart ratelinear interpolationAbout one value per second; between two values the heart rate changes smoothly.
Heart-rate mask0 if nearest real value > 5 s3 sessions (w05, w10, w18) have no heart-rate file. We mark the gap; we never invent values.
ParallelismapplyInPandas, one task per sessionSessions are independent, so each one can run on its own core.

Measured runtime of the full job: 57.7 s on 1 core, 40.9 s on 2, 37.1 s on 4. Two cores help; after that the job waits on reading files rather than on computing. Spark's automatic partition merging was turned off because it was putting all 21 sessions in one task.

3

One skeleton for every source

MM-Fit gives 18 joints (OpenPose order), EC3D gives 25 (in 3D), MediaPipe gives 33. All three are mapped to the same 18 joints, with y pointing up. MediaPipe has no neck point, so the neck is the midpoint of the two shoulders.

Both people end up identical: same origin, same size.
StepRuleWhy
Originmid-hip (average of the two hips) at (0, 0), every frameWhere the person stands in the image no longer matters.
Scaledivide by the median neck-to-hip distance of the windowHeight and distance to the camera no longer matter. The median ignores a few bad frames.
Resultinvariant to moving and resizing (tested)The model learns the movement, not the person or the camera.

Model 1: which exercise

InputOne window: pose 90 × 36, and/or watch 90 × 6.
Output11 probabilities (softmax): 10 exercises + no activity.
Codesrc/awc/models/m1_recognition.py, src/awc/training/train_m1.py.
EncoderShapeWhy we test it
1D-CNN3 blocks: convolution (kernel 5) → batch norm → ReLU → pooling; 64, 128, 128 channelsFinds short motion patterns; fast baseline.
LSTM2 layers, bidirectional, 64 units each wayReads the window in order and remembers what came before.
Transformer64 dimensions, 4 attention heads, 2 layers, learned time positionsEach moment can look at every other moment of the window: good for whole-movement patterns.
Training parameterValueWhy
OptimizerAdam, learning rate 0.001Standard, stable default for small networks.
Batch, epochs128, at most 30Fits easily on a laptop CPU.

Best: Transformer on camera + watch, macro-F1 0.880 (target 0.85), accuracy 0.950, 166,283 parameters, 24.7 minutes of CPU training. The camera-only Transformer (0.836, 84,811 parameters) is the one used on videos. Weakest classes: dumbbell rows (F1 0.59), squats (0.71), sit-ups (0.79); the other eight are above 0.90.

6

Rep counter

Not a neural network: signal processing on the normalized skeleton. It runs once the exercise is known, and also gives the start and end of every rep, which Model 3 will use.

Exercise groupSignal followedWhy
Squats, lunges, push-upsankle height relative to the hipsDrops once per rep whichever leg is in front.
Curls, rows, lateral raiseseach wrist relative to its shoulder, per armAlternating arms are counted separately and added.
Other exercisesmain direction of the whole body (PCA)General fallback when no body part dominates.
ParameterValueWhy
Smoothing9 frames (0.3 s)Removes skeleton jitter without hiding a rep.
Minimum gap0.6 s between repsNo human rep is faster; prevents counting one rep twice.
Prominence0.3 × the signal's spreadA peak must be a real movement, not a small wobble.
Peaks or valleysthe more regular oneWorks whether the exercise starts at the top or the bottom.

The bug we found and fixed. The first version always followed the whole body. On lunges and curls, which alternate left and right, it saw one left-plus-right cycle as one rep and counted exactly half. Average error over 616 MM-Fit sets: 1.35 → 0.49 reps (target 0.50); lunges 3.10 → 0.02, curls 5.07 → 0.46. The signal per exercise was chosen on those same sets, so the public videos (part 7) are the independent check.

Gym recorder

PhoneWeb app in Chrome on the Poco X3 Pro (docs/gym/index.html, served by GitHub Pages because the camera and Bluetooth need https).
WatchGalaxy Watch 7 running the free app Heart for Bluetooth, which sends heart rate with the standard Bluetooth heart-rate service. Pulsoid is the fallback.
SavedOne video per set, plus a session file: heart rate for the whole session, each set's exercise, reps, effort 1–10 and form.
ChoiceWhy
One clock: the phone'sVideo frames, heart rate and set marks are stamped by the same clock, so they line up without a sync step.
Heart rate during rests tooHow fast the heart rate recovers between sets is a fatigue signal for Model 3.
One video per setSmall files (a few MB) and no long recording held in the phone's memory.
Labels at the sourceExercise, reps, effort and form are typed right after each set, while we remember them.
Import scriptscripts/import_phone_session.py turns the downloads into the project's recording format. Checked on a real Chrome recording: 30.2 fps, frame clock within one frame (33 ms).
9

Limits and what comes next

  • Squats are the weakest class on unseen people (F1 0.71): standing still between reps looks like no activity or the start of a squat.
  • The rep counter's settings were chosen on MM-Fit itself; the public videos are the independent check.
  • No injury labels exist anywhere: Model 3 will predict fatigue and form breakdown, the warning signs.
  • Own recordings are only starting: they are the data for Model 3 and for the real-device test (RQ4).

Next validation: Model 2 on EC3D (correct rep or which mistake), Kafka streaming of camera and watch, our own sessions through Models 1 and 2, and the Spark history with personal baselines.

Glossary

Skeleton
The (x, y) positions of 18 body joints in one video frame.
Window
3 seconds of skeletons (90 frames): one input of Model 1.
Softmax
Turns the model's raw scores into probabilities that add up to 100 %.
Macro-F1
F1 balances precision and recall; macro = averaged over classes, so small classes count as much as big ones.
MAE
Mean absolute error: on average, how many reps the counter is off per set.
Unseen people
Test sessions of people who were not in the training data.
Leakage
Test information sneaking into training; avoided by computing statistics on training data only.
Early stopping
Stop training when the validation score has not improved for 5 epochs; keep the best epoch.
Parquet
Column-based file format that Spark reads and writes efficiently.
applyInPandas
Spark runs a normal pandas function on each group (here, each session) in parallel.
Prominence
How much a peak stands out from its surroundings; small wobbles have low prominence.
MediaPipe
Google's free pretrained pose estimator; the only pretrained part of our system.
