Conclusion

The ST-GCN's squat lead over the 2D random forest (55.6% vs 53.8%) is within the noise of the four leave-one-person-out folds (std 12.4 and 6.1). The random forest is better on lunge, plank and overall accuracy at 45°. Do not treat the ST-GCN as the winner of this benchmark.

Context only: the EC3D authors report 90.9% with their graph network, on a different input and setup, so it is not a target for this table. An earlier 3D random forest reached 53% on squats.

Mistake detection at 45°

Positive class = "not correct". Specificity = share of correct reps recognised as correct. A model that always says "mistake" gets recall 100% and specificity 0%.
