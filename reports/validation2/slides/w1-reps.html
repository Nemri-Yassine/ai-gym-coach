The signal per exercise was chosen on the same 616 sets, so these numbers are slightly optimistic; the public videos are the independent check.

We present the bug openly: the first counter halved lunges and curls because they alternate left and right. Using ankle height for leg exercises and each wrist separately for arm exercises fixed it. Lunges went from 3.10 to 0.02 reps of error.
